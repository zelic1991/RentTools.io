# Property-management target

STATUS: DRAFT
VERIFIED_AT: 2026-08-26
SCOPE: Generic product target; no named customer or market lead
CONTACT_SENT: NO

## Target user

Small short-term-rental operators with one to several properties who need one
place for reservations, calendar synchronization, guest communication,
operational assignments, and owner-controlled reporting.

The target includes single-property hosts, multi-unit buildings, co-hosts, and
local operators. It does not assume that every target needs full property
management or a marketplace.

## Product promise to test

Reduce fragmented calendar and guest-operation work while keeping the owner in
control of data, channels, permissions, and external registrations.

## Phases

### Phase 1: reliable core

- property and reservation model
- iCal import/export with loop and duplicate safeguards
- owner and manager permissions
- mobile operational view
- export, deletion, and audit visibility

### Phase 2: guest operations

- reservation-scoped guest forms
- message templates with explicit language selection
- check-in/out and turnover coordination
- optional document extraction with strict retention rules

### Phase 3: country-aware operations

- Croatia-specific fields and workflows only where requirements are verified
- external-system handoff and evidence status
- future registration-number readiness

### Phase 4: controlled commercial expansion

- direct enquiry workflow
- owner reporting and channel performance
- integrations only where provider access and terms permit

## Non-goals

- No public customer or lead directory.
- No automatic legal or tax advice.
- No assumption that an iCal feed is real-time.
- No claim that the product replaces Booking.com, Airbnb, eVisitor, or a
  professional channel manager in every operating model.
